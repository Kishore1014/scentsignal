Our story

Built by a
fragrance lover
for everyone

ScentSignal started from a simple frustration - finding the best UK price for a fragrance meant opening five different tabs, comparing manually, and still not being sure you got the best deal.

We are fragrance enthusiasts. We know Armaf Club de Nuit is one of the greatest value buys ever made. We know the UK market can vary by £30 or more for the exact same bottle.

So we built the tool we always wanted - one search, every UK retailer, sorted by price. Simple.

How we work

Honest by design

We compare prices across Amazon UK, eBay, Superdrug, Boots, Fragrance Direct, The Perfume Shop, Lookfantastic, John Lewis and more - with new retailers added regularly.

Every product page includes fragrance notes, scent profiles, longevity and sillage - so you make an informed decision, not just a cheap one. The fragrance community deserves better tools.

On affiliate links: When you click through to a retailer and buy, we may earn a small commission. This costs you nothing - the price you see is the price you pay. Commissions keep this site free. We never inflate prices or favour one retailer - cheapest always goes first, every time.
